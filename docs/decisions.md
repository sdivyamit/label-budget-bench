| D16 | 2026-11-10 | **Metric conventions.** Calibration is top-label (confidence = max(p, 1−p)). ECE primary = 10 equal-width bins (as in the BBS paper, for comparability); 15 equal-mass bins reported as robustness (was the plan's primary). | D17 | 2026-11-10 | **Break-even definition = Pecher et al. (EMNLP 2025), open item 2.** N⋆_mean: first N from which seed-mean q ≥ q_ref holds at every larger N in the sweep.
